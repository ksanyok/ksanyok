## What I do

I build practical products that sit between business needs and engineering depth: custom WordPress plugins, AI-driven SEO and content tools, migration systems, internal dashboards, messaging platforms, and niche utilities.

Across my public repositories, the recurring pattern is clear: I do not just ship templates. I design systems that automate workflows, reduce manual work, and turn domain problems into maintainable products.

## Where my public work points

- **WordPress and WooCommerce engineering**: custom plugins for SEO analysis, ACF-based content management, pricing workflows, product UX, translation automation, and shipping logic.
- **AI and language tooling**: offline-first text processing, AI detection, paraphrasing, tone analysis, readability, and multilingual content pipelines.
- **Full-stack TypeScript platforms**: backend services, queues, real-time updates, admin panels, and production deployment.
- **Migration and modernization**: CLI tooling to move legacy CMS content into modern Astro-based sites with repeatable deployment workflows.
- **Product utilities**: parcel tracking, power monitoring, Telegram bots, and macOS conversation assistants.
